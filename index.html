Section 03

Events

At every industry conference, TrialHub activates with geo-targeted Meta ads and a three-tier creative strategy designed to filter for the right buyer.

Geo-Targeted Meta Ads

The 1km radius around the venue is the active targeting zone, where attendees are on the convention floor and nearby. The 1km to 2km outer ring is excluded to avoid wasting budget on people who are not at the event.

Component 3: Data and Personalisation

Generic outreach gets ignored. Personalised outreach starts conversations. The difference is research depth and message specificity.

Generic: What to avoid
Hi FirstName, I'm Ivan with TrialHub. We work with sponsors like Novartis and Bayer to provide country-level drug reimbursement data to help them plan better. Are you open to having a conversation to see if there is a fit at Company?
Personalised: What to aim for
Hi Nia,

The regulatory calendar at Vertex this year is intense: povetacicept BLA, CASGEVY, and ALYFTREK pediatric submissions, JOURNAVX in Canada, all in the first half alone.

When you are filing across that many geographies simultaneously, how is your team benchmarking country-level regulatory start-up timelines?

At TrialHub, we provide country-by-country reimbursement intelligence and regulatory start-up data across 70 plus markets, helping regulatory teams sequence global filings based on real-world access timelines, not assumptions. The variance between countries is often wider than internal estimates suggest.

Are you open to learning more in a short conversation?
